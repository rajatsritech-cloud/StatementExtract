## Introduction: Why Wave Accounting Users Rely on CSV Uploads

Wave Accounting ([waveapps.com](https://www.waveapps.com)) is a popular cloud bookkeeping platform designed specifically for freelancers, consultants, sole proprietors, and small business owners. Its clean, accessible interface and free core accounting tools make it an attractive alternative to complex enterprise software.

However, Wave's automated bank connection service (powered by Plaid) frequently encounters connection timeouts or security MFA failures with smaller credit unions, regional banks, and non-US financial institutions. When automated connections fail, business owners must manually upload bank statements.

Wave does not accept PDF statements or complex `.QBO` files for manual upload. **Wave strictly requires clean, specifically formatted `.CSV` files**. Uploading a CSV with an unrecognized column layout, missing headers, or inverted numbers will either crash the uploader or create duplicate and reversed transactions in your Chart of Accounts.

This guide provides the exact CSV formatting templates required by Wave Accounting, diagnoses common import errors, and explains how to convert bank PDF statements into Wave-ready CSVs in seconds.

## The Official Wave Accounting CSV Specifications

Wave supports two standard CSV layout options: the **3-Column Format** and the **4-Column Format**. Both formats require specific column header naming conventions.

### Option 1: The 3-Column CSV Format (Single Signed Amount)

This is the simplest and most reliable format for Wave. Money leaving your account (expenses, withdrawals) is represented as a **negative number**, and money entering your account (deposits, income) is represented as a **positive number**.

```csv
Date,Description,Amount
2026-03-02,Client Payment ACME Corp,1250.00
2026-03-05,Office Depot Supplies,-84.20
2026-03-08,Coffee Meeting Client,-14.50
2026-03-15,Stripe Merchant Settlement,3420.80
2026-03-22,Google Workspace Subscription,-12.00
```

### Option 2: The 4-Column CSV Format (Separated Inflow & Outflow)

If your bank statement separates deposits and withdrawals into two distinct columns, use Wave's 4-column layout:

```csv
Date,Description,Deposit,Withdrawal
2026-03-02,Client Payment ACME Corp,1250.00,
2026-03-05,Office Depot Supplies,,84.20
2026-03-08,Coffee Meeting Client,,14.50
2026-03-15,Stripe Merchant Settlement,3420.80,
2026-03-22,Google Workspace Subscription,,12.00
```

> **Crucial Rule for Option 2**: In the 4-column format, both `Deposit` and `Withdrawal` columns must contain positive numbers. Do not include negative signs in the `Withdrawal` column; Wave automatically registers the `Withdrawal` column as money spent.

## Formatting Rules Enforced by Wave

Before uploading your CSV to Wave, ensure your file satisfies these strict technical criteria:

| Parameter | Wave Requirement | Invalid Example | Valid Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Date Format** | `YYYY-MM-DD`, `MM/DD/YYYY`, or `DD/MM/YYYY` | `March 4th, 2026` | `2026-03-04` |
| **Currency Symbols** | **None allowed** | `$1,250.00` | `1250.00` |
| **Thousands Comma** | **None allowed** | `1,250.00` | `1250.00` |
| **Header Names** | Case-insensitive standard names | `Posting_Date`, `Narrative` | `Date`, `Description`, `Amount` |
| **File Encoding** | `UTF-8` | `Windows-1252` | `UTF-8` |
| **Max File Size** | 5 MB | > 5 MB | Split into multi-month files |

## Step-by-Step: How to Upload a Bank Statement CSV into Wave

Follow these steps to import your transactions into Wave:

```text
[ Wave Dashboard ]
        │
        ▼
[ Accounting > Transactions ]
        │
        ▼
[ Click "More" or "Upload a bank statement" ]
        │
        ▼
[ Choose Target Bank / Credit Card Account ]
        │
        ▼
[ Select your validated .CSV File ]
        │
        ▼
[ Confirm Column Mapping & Date Format ]
        │
        ▼
[ Click "Upload" & Categorize in Transactions Screen ]
```

### Detailed Instructions:
1. Log into your account at [waveapps.com](https://www.waveapps.com).
2. In the left navigation menu, click **Accounting > Transactions**.
3. In the upper-right corner, click the **More** button (or **Upload a statement**).
4. In the pop-up modal, select the destination account from the **Account** dropdown (e.g., *Business Checking* or *Corporate Credit Card*).
5. Click **Browse** and select your formatted `.csv` file.
6. If your CSV headers do not match automatically, Wave's mapping interface will prompt you to designate which columns represent `Date`, `Description`, and `Amount`.
7. Choose the matching **Date Format** dropdown (e.g., `YYYY-MM-DD`).
8. Click **Upload**.
9. Wave will parse the file and display all new transactions in your **Transactions** feed with an orange "Unreviewed" marker, ready for 1-click categorization against your Chart of Accounts.

## Troubleshooting Common Wave Upload Errors

### Error 1: "We couldn't upload your file. Please check that your file is formatted correctly"

**The Cause**: The CSV contains currency symbols (`$`, `€`, `£`) or thousands separators (`,`). Wave's numeric parser cannot interpret commas within numbers because it uses the comma as a delimiter.

**The Solution**:
1. Open the CSV in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.
2. Select the amount column, right-click, and choose **Format Cells > Number**.
3. Set decimal places to `2` and uncheck "Use 1000 Separator (,)".
4. Re-save as standard CSV.

---

### Error 2: Inverted Income and Expenses

**The Cause**: On credit card statements or inverted checking exports, payments made were exported as positive numbers and purchases as negative numbers.

**The Solution**:
- In Wave, expenses must reduce your cash balance (represented by negative numbers in the 3-column format).
- If your transactions imported with reversed signs, delete the imported batch (`Accounting > Transactions > Filter by Account > Select All > Delete`) and flip the signs using Statement Extract before re-uploading.

---

### Error 3: Duplicate Transaction Dates from Overlapping Uploads

**The Cause**: Unlike enterprise software that tracks unique transaction hash IDs, Wave relies on simple deduplication based on Date + Amount + Description. If you upload overlapping date ranges with slight narrative variations, duplicate entries will appear in your ledger.

**The Solution**:
Always ensure your statement date ranges do not overlap. If you previously uploaded statements through March 15, ensure your next CSV begins strictly on March 16.
