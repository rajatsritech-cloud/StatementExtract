Parse Email Attachments
What is Parsing?
Parsing is the process of analyzing data from structured or semi-structured sources and transforming it into a format your applications or workflows can use. In the context of emails, email data extraction allows you to extract email attachments automatically and feed the content directly into your CRM, ERP, or spreadsheet systems.

Parsing saves hours of repetitive work, reducing errors while enabling teams to focus on strategic tasks. For more details, see our Parsing Definition article.

What is Email Attachment Parsing?
Email attachment parsing is specifically designed to handle the files attached to your emails. It enables automatic processing of PDFs, CSVs, Excel sheets, Word documents, and images. By extracting critical data automatically, companies can maintain accurate records and reduce manual intervention.


How to Use It Step by Step
Step 1: Choose and Connect Your Email Source
Select the email account you want to parse. Most tools support Gmail, Outlook, and IMAP-compatible accounts. Once connected, the parser can access incoming emails securely to automatically pull data from email to Excel or other destinations.

Step 2: Define Document Types to Parse
Identify the types of documents relevant to your workflow. Finance teams may focus on invoices in PDF or CSV format, while HR departments may prioritize resumes in DOCX or PDF. Clear document definitions help ensure accurate extraction.

Step 3: Create Parsing Rules
Parsing rules determine which data fields are extracted from attachments. For example, you can define rules to capture invoice numbers, dates, amounts, or client names. Some advanced parsers even allow testing rules on sample emails before deployment.

Step 4: Define Data Destination
After extraction, decide where the data should go. Many companies extract data from Gmail to Google Sheets, Excel, or cloud storage. Automating this step ensures data is immediately usable for reporting, accounting, or workflow automation.

Step 5: Test and Automate
Always test your parsing rules on a sample batch of emails. Verify that the extracted data is accurate, then activate automation to free your team from repetitive manual tasks.

Supported Formats for Email Attachment Parsing
Parsing software supports a wide range of file types to accommodate various business needs.

1. Text and Office Documents
PDF: Common for invoices, reports, and scanned documents; often includes OCR for scanned content.
DOC / DOCX: Word documents for contracts, HR forms, and reports.
TXT: Simple text files for logs or automated reports.
RTF: Less common but still supported in some workflows.
2. Spreadsheets and Structured Data
XLS / XLSX: Microsoft Excel spreadsheets for financial and operational data.
CSV: Easy-to-import tabular data.
ODS: OpenDocument Spreadsheet for LibreOffice users.
3. Images and Multimedia
JPG / JPEG, PNG, BMP, TIFF: Scanned receipts, forms, or photos.
