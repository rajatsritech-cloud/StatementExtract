## Introduction: TD Bank US vs. TD Canada Trust

Toronto-Dominion Bank (TD Bank Group) is one of the largest multinational financial conglomerates in North America. Unlike regional US banks, TD operates two major, distinct banking subsidiaries:

1. **TD Bank, N.A. (America's Most Convenient Bank)**: Headquartered in Cherry Hill, New Jersey, serving the US East Coast from Maine to Florida. Statements use US banking standards (9-digit ABA routing numbers, USD currency, and US MM/DD date notation).
2. **TD Canada Trust**: Headquartered in Toronto, Ontario, serving consumers and commercial enterprises across Canada. Statements use Canadian Payments Association standards (5-digit transit number, 3-digit financial institution number `004`, CAD currency, and bilingual English/French formatting).

Because cross-border businesses, Canadian snowbirds, and multinational corporations frequently maintain accounts on both sides of the border, bookkeepers must handle both statement formats seamlessly.

This guide provides an end-to-end walkthrough of converting both TD Bank US and TD Canada Trust statement PDFs into formatted Excel spreadsheets, CSV files, and QuickBooks Web Connect files.

## Technical Challenges When Parsing TD Statements

### 1. Bilingual Text Noise Removal (Canadian Statements)
On TD Canada Trust statements, column headers and standard transaction descriptions include slash-delimited bilingual translations:
`WITHDRAWALS / RETRAITS`, `BALANCE / SOLDE`, `INTERAC E-TRANSFER / VIREMENT INTERAC`.
- A generic OCR tool outputs the slash-separated text, causing messy, bloated strings in your general ledger.
- An intelligent financial parser strips the secondary language noise, isolating clean vendor names (`Rogers Cable`, `Revenue Canada`) for clean accounting bank rules.

### 2. Month-Name Date Parsing (`01 MAR` vs `2026-03-01`)
TD Canada Trust statements often print dates as day followed by a 3-letter English/French month abbreviation:
`01 MAR`, `15 AVR`, `22 MAI`.
- If simply exported to Excel, Excel may fail to recognize `15 AVR` (Avril / April) as a valid calendar date, leading to sorting errors.
- The extraction engine detects the statement year from the header block, translates the multilingual month token, and normalizes each row into standard ISO format: `2026-04-15`.

### 3. Check Image Extraction and Check Registers
TD Bank statements frequently include check images on subsequent pages:
- These pages display scanned front-and-back thumbnails of cleared checks.
- A quality converter ignores the embedded image pages and extracts only the verified numeric check register, preventing duplicate rows or optical hallucination errors.

## Step-by-Step: Converting TD Statements to Excel

Follow these steps to convert your TD Bank statements into clean spreadsheets:

### Step 1: Download Statements from TD EasyWeb or TD Online Banking
- **TD Bank US**: Log into [td.com](https://www.td.com) > Select Account > Statements > Download PDF.
- **TD Canada Trust**: Log into [EasyWeb Internet Banking](https://easyweb.td.com) > Select Account > Statements > Save PDF.

### Step 2: Upload to Statement Extract

### Step 3: Check Mathematical Continuity
The tool validates that:
$$\text{Starting Balance} - \text{Total Subtractions} + \text{Total Additions} = \text{Ending Balance}$$
A green confirmation badge guarantees zero omitted rows.

### Step 4: Export to Excel, CSV, or QuickBooks
Choose your desired export format:
- **Microsoft Excel (.xlsx)**: Complete with formulas, date formats, and clean numerical columns.
- **QuickBooks (.qbo)**: Formatted for 1-click Web Connect bank feed ingestion into QuickBooks Online or Desktop.
